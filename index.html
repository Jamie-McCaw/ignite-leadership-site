You already have what it takes. It needs to be ignited.

The IGNITE framework is a practical, repeatable system that helps you unlock your leadership potential one intentional step at a time. It gives you a clear path from where you are to where you want to be.

I

Identify leadership moments

Recognize where leadership is already happening

Leadership isn't just about the big decisions or formal authority. It's happening in everyday moments: when you choose to speak up in a meeting, when you give feedback that helps someone grow, when you model the behavior you want to see. Most leaders miss opportunities to lead because they're waiting for a title, a promotion, or "the right time."

In practice
  • Identify 3 to 5 micro-leadership moments happening in your current role each week
  • Recognize patterns in when you naturally step into leadership
  • Understand the difference between managing and leading

Result

You stop waiting for permission to lead and start showing up as a leader today.

G

Grow one skill at a time

Focus your development for maximum impact

You don't need to fix everything at once. Trying to improve in ten areas simultaneously guarantees you'll improve in none of them. We help you identify the one skill that will have the greatest ripple effect across your leadership and build a focused development plan around it.

In practice
  • Complete assessments to identify your highest-leverage growth area
  • Create a 30 to 90 day skill development plan with clear milestones
  • Practice the skill in real-world situations with coaching support
  • Master it before moving to the next skill

Result

Tangible, measurable improvement instead of surface-level change.

N

Navigate the messy middle

Push through when growth gets uncomfortable

Leadership development is uncomfortable. You'll try new approaches that feel awkward. You'll have conversations you've been avoiding. You'll make mistakes. Most leaders quit here because they mistake discomfort for failure. We help you through this phase with support, accountability, and the reminder that this awkwardness is progress.

In practice
  • Expect and normalize the discomfort of growth
  • Use coaching support when you want to revert to old patterns
  • Celebrate small wins and track incremental progress
  • Develop resilience in your leadership approach

Result

You build the emotional muscle to sustain change rather than abandon it when it gets hard.

I

Implement immediately

Turn insights into action without delay

Implementation is where theory becomes transformation. We don't let you leave any session without a clear action plan for what you'll do differently this week. Immediate, imperfect action beats delayed perfection every time.

In practice
  • End every coaching session with specific actions for the next 7 days
  • Start small: one conversation, one meeting, one decision approached differently
  • Report back on what you implemented and what you learned
  • Adjust and iterate based on real-world results

Result

Your calendar reflects your leadership commitments, not just your intentions.

T

Track your evolution

Measure progress and maintain accountability

You can't improve what you don't track. We help you set clear measures for your leadership growth, both quantitative (assessment feedback scores, team engagement, retention) and qualitative (how conversations feel different, moments of increased confidence). Tracking isn't about judgment. It shows you what's working, what needs adjustment, and how far you've come.

In practice
  • Establish baseline measurements before starting development
  • Use weekly reflection questions to capture qualitative progress
  • Conduct quarterly check-ins with stakeholders for an external perspective
  • Review and celebrate wins while identifying next growth edges

Result

You have proof of progress and data to guide continued development.

E

Elevate others

Your leadership is measured by who you lift up

The ultimate test of leadership isn't what you accomplish, it's who you develop. We help you move from "How do I lead better?" to "How do I help others lead better?" This is where your individual development creates organizational impact.

In practice
  • Identify emerging leaders you can mentor or sponsor
  • Share the frameworks and tools you've found valuable
  • Create leadership opportunities for others on your team
  • Build a culture where leadership development becomes contagious

Result

Your legacy becomes the leaders you develop, not just the results you achieve.

IGNITE isn't linear; it's cyclical. As you master one skill and elevate others, you'll identify new leadership moments that require growth in new areas. The cycle continues, deepening your impact and expanding your influence.
